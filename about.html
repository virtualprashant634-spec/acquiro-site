Two and a half minutes: the product tour

Why I built Acquiro.

Watch the founder's two-minute pitch on YouTube

I'm a developer in Austin. I was a realtor before that. Agents and wholesalers send me deals every week, each one with an after-repair value I can't trust. Checking one by hand takes 45 minutes, so most never heard back, and agents who never hear back stop sending deals.

So I built the acquisitions team I needed: it reads the deal, finds the lot, the zoning, the flood map and the tree canopy on its own, pulls closed new-build comps within half a mile, runs two homes against three, and answers the agent with the number.

If the money clears my buy box it comes to me as a Review with the weak spots flagged. If it doesn't, the agent still gets a real answer, a Decline with the max I'd pay. Nobody waits a week for silence.

Prashant Vanka
