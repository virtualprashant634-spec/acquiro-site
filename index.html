Questions

Where do the comps come from?

Closed new-build sales within half a mile of the lot. When too few closed nearby, it widens to the ZIP and marks the result low confidence.

Does it ever send?

Yes, a Decline sends its reply on its own, in your voice, with the max you would pay. A Review waits for you, and offers sit behind a button on the desk.

What do you buy?

Whatever clears the buy box you teach it. That means minimum profit per lot, margin on cost, return on equity, cash-on-cash, lot size and ZIPs.

Who is behind it?

I was a realtor and I hated the silence, so I built the thing that answers. Replies go out under my name, signed Prashant.

What does it cost?

Send one deal first. It costs nothing to see what comes back. Team pricing is coming.

Can it work for my market?

Austin is what runs today. Other markets and asset types are coming as new buy boxes on the same engine.
