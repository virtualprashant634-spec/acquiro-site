The problem

Good lots get lost in the inbox.

Deal emails arrive all day from wholesalers, InvestorLift blasts and brokers. Each one claims an after-repair value. Checking it by hand takes time, so agents often never hear back. Agents who never hear back stop sending deals.

How it works

From deal email to decision, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Forward the deal.

    Send the InvestorLift blast or broker email to prashant+deals@avivo.homes, or fill in the deal form. The desk reads the text, the flyer or the MLS sheet and fills in one standard deal record. Every fact is tagged “agent sent” or “we found.”

    agent sentwe found
  2. 02

    It checks the comps.

    The desk pulls independent closed new-build sales from MLS sold data, within 0.25 to 0.5 mile of the lot, and sets their price per square foot beside the number the wholesaler claimed. Their after-repair value is shown, but never used in the math.

    Closed compsClaimed
  3. 03

    You get a verdict.

    It works out the profit per lot for a 2-house and a 3-house plan. At $600k or more profit per lot and a 20% margin on cost, the deal is flagged Surface for you. If not, a 3-bullet reply with the real numbers and the price that works waits in your Gmail drafts.

What it does

The due diligence, done before you open it.

  • Reads any deal email

    Plain text, PDF flyers, MLS sheets and InvestorLift blasts become one standard deal record.

  • Facts you can trace

    Every field shows where it came from: “agent sent” or “we found.” Lot size and zoning are looked up on their own, not copied from the flyer.

  • Independent sold comps

    Closed new-build sales from MLS sold data, within 0.25 to 0.5 mile of the lot and over the last 12 months, with the count, median, low and high. Under 3 comps, it says “insufficient comps.” It never passes a deal on thin data.

  • Judged per lot

    2 houses or 3, whichever makes more. Profit is measured on the lot, not on the house or on a percentage.

  • Surface or draft

    A Surface flag when a lot clears $600k profit and a 20% margin on cost. Otherwise a 3-bullet Gmail reply with the price that works.

One engine, swappable packs

Built for lots. Ready for the next asset class.

The engine is the same for every deal: email in, standard record, facts checked, value worked out, scored, then a flag or a draft. Only the asset pack changes. A pack holds four things.

  1. Buy box rules.

    The gates a deal must pass: where, how big, how strong the comps.

  2. Value method.

    How a deal is valued. For lots: sold new-build comps, price per square foot.

  3. Comp sources.

    Where the comps come from. For lots: an MLS sold export now, a live feed later.

  4. Scoring threshold.

    The line a deal must clear. For lots: $600k profit per lot and a 20% margin on cost.

You stay in charge

It does the homework. You make the call.

  • Nothing sends without a click.

    Replies are drafts in your Gmail. The desk has no send button.

  • Pricing is your call.

    The desk shows the math and the price that works. You decide.

  • Missing facts become questions.

    If the email leaves something out, the draft asks the agent for it.

  • No guessing.

    A weak address match comes back “not found.” The wholesaler’s after-repair value is shown, never trusted.
